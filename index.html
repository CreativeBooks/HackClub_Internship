How to Access NASA API:

I

Go to api.nasa.gov

II

Complete the registration form with your first name, last name, and email address

III

Your unique API key will be sent to your email address. This key is a string of letters and numbers that authenticates your requests. A generic DEMO_KEY is available for initial testing, but you should use your personal key for expanded usage and to avoid rate limits.
